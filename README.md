# Apex Planet Data Analytics Internship – Task 3

## Deep-Dive Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

This project is part of the **Apex Planet Data Analytics Internship**.

The objective of Task 3 was to perform deeper business analysis on the sales dataset and develop an **interactive Power BI dashboard** to analyze sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance, category performance, and regional sales.

---

## Project Objective

The main objectives of this task were:

- Perform deep-dive business analysis
- Analyze overall revenue and sales performance
- Analyze category-wise performance
- Analyze product-wise performance
- Analyze city-wise sales
- Analyze customer behaviour
- Perform customer segmentation
- Analyze gender-wise orders
- Identify monthly revenue trends
- Develop meaningful KPIs
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate actionable business insights

---

## Dataset

The analysis was performed using the cleaned sales dataset prepared during the previous task.

The dataset contains sales-related information including:

- Customer information

# Deep-Dive Analysis

## 1. Revenue & Sales Performance

Overall sales performance was analyzed to understand the business's revenue generation and order activity.

Important KPIs were developed to provide a quick overview of business performance.

---

## 2. Category Performance

Sales and revenue were analyzed across different product categories.

**Key Finding:**

- Electronics generated the highest category revenue.
- Electronics also had the highest number of orders.

This indicates that Electronics was one of the strongest-performing categories in the dataset.

---

## 3. Product Performance

Product-level analysis was performed to identify products contributing significantly to overall revenue.

**Key Finding:**

- Laptop was one of the leading products by revenue.

Product-level insights can help businesses improve inventory planning, promotions, and product-focused strategies.

---

## 4. City-wise Sales Analysis

Sales performance was compared across different cities to identify high-performing locations.

**Key Finding:**

- Patna recorded the highest city-wise sales.

This helps identify regions where successful sales strategies can potentially be studied and replicated.

---

## 5. Customer Analysis

Customer-level analysis was performed to understand customer contribution and behaviour.

The analysis considered:

- Customer revenue contribution
- Customer purchasing behaviour
- Customer segments
- Product preferences
- Category preferences

---

## 6. Customer Segmentation

Customer segmentation was performed to identify differences in customer groups based on their contribution and behaviour.

This analysis helps businesses understand:

- High-value customers
- Different customer groups
- Revenue contribution
- Customer preferences
- Potential targeting opportunities

---

## 7. Gender Analysis

Customer orders and sales were analyzed based on gender.

The analysis showed a relatively similar distribution between male and female customers.

Gender was therefore considered as one of several customer attributes rather than the only factor for customer targeting.

---

## 8. Monthly Revenue Trends

Monthly revenue was analyzed to identify changes in sales performance over time.

**Key Finding:**

- Monthly revenue showed fluctuations across the year.

Trend analysis can help businesses understand changes in demand and identify periods requiring additional attention.

---

The dashboard provides a visual overview of sales performance and allows users to explore different business dimensions interactively.

---

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard allows users to filter and explore the data based on:

- City
- Category
- Product

These filters make it easier to perform focused analysis and compare different business segments.

# Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several important insights:

- **Electronics** generated the highest category revenue.
- **Electronics** had the highest number of orders.
- **Laptop** was one of the leading products by revenue.
- **Patna** recorded the highest city-wise sales.
- Monthly revenue fluctuated across the year.
- Customer segments showed differences in revenue contribution.
- Male and female customers had a relatively similar distribution.
